⚔️ Pendragon Pool Generator

Community MTG Tournament — Simply Cyber Edition

📜 How It Works

  • Click Generate My Pool to receive your personal random selection of 15 common creatures (your Arthur candidates) and 10 legendary equipments (your Excalibur candidates).
  • Pick one Arthur and one Excalibur — these go in your command zone.
  • Build a 98-card singleton deck using any legal common cards (all 98 must be common).
  • Your deck's color identity = the combined colors of your Arthur + Excalibur.
  • Starting life: 30  |  Commander damage: 21
  • Bring your finished deck to the event. Good luck! ⚔️

🤝 Honor System: This is your pool — no rerolling to fish for better picks! Choose one Arthur and one Excalibur, then go build something awesome.

⚔️ Your Arthurs — Pick One Common Creature

✨ Your Excaliburs — Pick One Legendary Equipment

This legendary equipment starts in your command zone. It can be any rarity. Click any card to view it on Scryfall.
